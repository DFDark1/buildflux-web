From mud to mug

Clay takes weeks, not minutes. Here is the path every piece takes through the studio.

Wedge

Knead the clay to push out air bubbles so it won’t crack in the kiln.

Throw

Center the clay on the wheel and pull it up into a form.

Trim

When it is leather-hard, carve the foot and clean the edges.

Bisque fire

A first slow firing turns soft clay into strong, porous ceramic.

Glaze

Dip, brush or pour. Glazes look chalky now and change completely in the kiln.

Glaze fire

The second firing melts the glaze into glass. Pick up your piece a week later.
